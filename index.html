Boss Breaker

An endless pixel block breaker. Six worlds, six bosses, one score.

Need help?

Email vampyrobusiness@gmail.com and describe what happened. Your device model and iOS version help a lot.

Common questions

My best score disappeared.
Scores are stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is synced. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, and so does offloading the app in iOS Storage settings.
The game feels like it is running at half speed.
Check whether Low Power Mode is on. iOS deliberately caps animation to 30 frames per second while it is enabled, in every app. Turning it off in Settings → Battery restores full speed.
How do the ads work?
A single full-screen ad appears between some runs — never during play, never as a banner. There is a minimum time gap so a bad streak does not turn into a wall of ads.
Is there anything to buy?
No. There are no purchases, no currencies, no lives, and no timers.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The game itself needs no connection; only the ads do.
What do the combo callouts mean?
They rank a single volley by how much work each ball did. LEGENDARY is the top of the ladder and lands on roughly one volley in seventy-seven.
