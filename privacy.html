Boss Breaker

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Boss Breaker asks you for nothing. No account, no sign-in, no email address, no analytics. It does show ads between some runs, and the ad network sees a little about your device. That is the only data that leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

Three values are saved locally and never transmitted anywhere:

WhatWhy
Your best scoreSo it survives closing the app
Whether sound is mutedSo the app opens the way you left it
Whether auto fast-forward is onSame

Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows a full-screen ad between some runs, served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google receives:

  • A device identifier, used to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure whether an ad worked
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information from the ad SDK
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, as with any internet request

We do not track you across other apps or websites. The app never requests App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your device's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking, which means the ads you see are non-personalised.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policy: Google's partner-sites policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, a consent form appears before any ads load, and you can change your answer at any time.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics or crash reporting beyond what the ad SDK reports to Google
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or health data
  • No purchases of any kind
  • No chat, no user-generated content, no links out of the game

Children

The app is rated for all ages and asks no personal information from anyone. Ad content is filtered to an age-appropriate rating. If you believe a child has been shown something inappropriate, contact us and we will tighten the setting.

Your choices

  • Turning ads off inside the app is not currently possible.
  • On iOS you can limit ad tracking system-wide under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored locally.

Changes

If a future version collects anything more, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
