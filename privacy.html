Privacy Policy — Boss Breaker

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Boss Breaker asks you for nothing. No account, no sign-in, no email address, no analytics. It does show ads between some runs, and the ad network sees a little about your device. That is the only data that leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

Three values are saved locally so the game behaves the way you left it. None of them are sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

WhatWhy
Your best scoreSo it is still there when you close the app
Whether sound is mutedSo the app opens the way you left it
Whether auto fast-forward is onSame reason

Advertising

Boss Breaker shows a full-screen ad between some runs, and an optional ad you can choose to watch in exchange for a powerup. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To do that, the ad SDK receives:

  • a device identifier
  • usage and diagnostic information about how the ad performed
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent form appears before any ad loads. It is provided by Google's own consent management platform, which is certified under the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework. Your answer decides what happens next:

  • If you consent, ads may be personalised using information Google already holds about your device.
  • If you decline, you still get ads, but they are non-personalised — chosen from general context rather than anything about you.

Everywhere else, ads are served under Google's standard terms for that region.

Boss Breaker does not track you across other apps or websites. On iOS the app never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt, which means it does not have access to your device's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking, on any setting.

What Google does with ad data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or email addresses
  • No analytics beyond what the ad SDK reports about its own ads
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or health data — the app never asks for any of these permissions
  • No purchases of any kind
  • No user-generated content, chat, or social features
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Children

Boss Breaker applies age-appropriate ad filtering. If you see an ad in the game that you do not think belongs there, email the address below and the settings will be tightened.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking at the system level. On iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove everything. Deleting the app deletes the three saved values listed above. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Boss Breaker collects anything beyond what is described here, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
